# Copyright & Fair Dealing Compliance Guidelines

This document outlines the legal and pedagogical framework governing music analyses, chord progressions, guitar tablature, and audio tools published on **MusicScene** (`musicscene.com.au`).

---

## 1. Legal Jurisdictional Framework

MusicScene operates under Australian law, specifically governed by the **_Copyright Act 1968_ (Cth)** of Australia, while harmonizing with international doctrines such as US **Fair Use (17 U.S.C. § 107)** and Berne Convention principles for global access.

## 2. Core Legal Principles by Content Component

| Component | Copyright Status | Legal Doctrine / Authority | MusicScene Implementation Rule |

Chords and harmonic sequences are the fundamental vocabulary and grammar of music; ideas and systems cannot be copyrighted. | Anyone may teach or analyze chord sequences (e.g., `E - A - B - C#m`, `I - IV - V - vi`, 12-bar blues). |

Never full commercial sheet music replacements. |

| **Lyrics** | **Fair Dealing Excerpt Only** | Print licensing applies to full lyrics. | Only quote brief phrases in quotes (e.g. *"Cheap wine and a three day growth"*) to identify song sections for the student. Never publish full lyric sheets. |

## 3. Fair Dealing under the Australian *Copyright Act 1968*

### Section 40: Fair Dealing for Purpose of Research or Study
- Content on MusicScene is strictly designed for individual self-instruction, instrumental practice, and music theory education.
- The "quantitative test" under s 40 favors small, non-substantial excerpts required to illustrate a pedagogical concept.

### Section 41: Fair Dealing for Purpose of Criticism or Review
- Section 41 permits dealing with a copyright work for criticism or review, provided there is **sufficient acknowledgement**.
- MusicScene explicitly attributes every analyzed work with:
  - Song title
  - Original songwriter / performing artist
  - Album and release year
  - Harmonic context and critical commentary on why the composition works

## 4. Distinction: MusicScene vs. Commercial Sheet Music / Full Tab Platforms

Commercial tab websites (such as Ultimate Guitar or Songsterr) publish full, end-to-end transcriptions of entire compositions and interactive backing tracks that act as substitutes for sheet music sales. Consequently, they negotiate blanket digital print licenses with publisher collection societies (e.g. **APRA AMCOS** and **Hal Leonard Australia**).

**MusicScene is distinct because:**
1. It does **not** provide full-length song scores or multi-track stems.
2. It does **not** offer downloadable Guitar Pro (`.gp5`, `.gpx`), PDF sheet music, or MIDI files of copyrighted compositions.
3. Every tablature snippet is explicitly framed as an **educational exercise** (e.g. *"Verse Boogie Shuffle Technique"*, *"Sliding 6ths Solo Study"*).
4. The site's primary value is educational analysis, technical instruction, and music theory.

## 5. Golden Rules for Future Song Analysis & Content Creation

Every contributor or automated workflow creating song analyses on MusicScene must adhere to these five rules:

1. **Excerpts Only (1–4 Measures)**:
   - Provide only the key signature lick, rhythm pattern, or solo climax needed to illustrate the technique.
   - Never transcribe the entire piece end-to-end.
2. **Exercise Framing**:
   - Always assign an `exerciseLabel` describing the technical skill (e.g., *"Double-Stop Bend Exercise"*, *"Palm-Muted Rhythmic Study"*).
3. **No Commercial Downloadable Files**:
   - Do not offer `.pdf`, `.mid`, or `.gp` downloadable score exports.
4. **No Master Audio**:
   - Rely solely on Web Audio browser synthesis, chord voicings, or embedded official YouTube/Spotify player embeds if relevant.
5. **Clear Attribution & Terms of Service**:
   - Always credit the composer and artist.
   - Maintain the Fair Dealing disclaimer in the site's [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service).
