# Facebook Business Page Privacy & Friend Isolation Checklist

This checklist guarantees that your personal Facebook friends, family, and colleagues never see your MusicScene posts, activity, or promotions.

---

## 1. Page vs. Personal Profile (The Core Rule)

* MusicScene must be operated as a **Facebook Business Page**, NOT posted from your personal timeline.
* Business Pages have their own standalone name, logo, followers, and URL.
* Facebook's algorithm **does not** push Page posts onto your personal friends' newsfeeds unless you manually share them to your personal wall.

---

## 2. Strict Privacy Checklist (5 Rules)

1. **Rule #1: Dismiss All "Invite Friends" Prompts**
   * Meta will repeatedly show notifications: *"Grow your page! Invite your 250 friends to like MusicScene"*.
   * **Always click Cancel / Dismiss.** Never invite personal friends.

2. **Rule #2: Never Share Page Posts to Your Personal Profile**
   * Keep the boundary absolute. Do not click "Share to Personal Feed".

3. **Rule #3: Use Meta Business Suite for Posting**
   * Install the **Meta Business Suite** app on iOS/Android, or bookmark `business.facebook.com` on desktop.
   * Posting from Business Suite operates exclusively under the `@MusicScene` business persona, preventing accidental personal profile mix-ups.

4. **Rule #4: Interacting in Guitar Groups as the Page**
   * In Facebook, look at the top right profile bubble and switch your active identity to **MusicScene**.
   * When you join guitar groups (e.g. *Guitar Players Australia*, *Classic Rock Guitarists*) and comment on questions, your personal name is completely hidden. Members only see "MusicScene".

5. **Rule #5: Audience Geofencing (Optional Extra Layer)**
   * In your Facebook Page Settings -> General -> **Country / Age Restrictions**, you can restrict visibility to specific regions or set age minimums if desired.
   * If you ever test $5 boosted posts or ads, Meta's targeting allows you to target specific interests (e.g., *Guitar*, *Fender*, *Music Theory*) without touching your personal friend graph.
