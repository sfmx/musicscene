## 🎸 5 Newly Queued Masterclasses

### 1. While My Guitar Gently Weeps — The Beatles (feat. Eric Clapton)
* **Key & Tempo:** A Minor • 66 BPM
* **The Secret:** The descending chromatic line cliché (A → G → F# → F) in the verse mimics human weeping, while the bridge bursts into parallel A Major (A - C#m - F#m) for stunning narrative contrast.
* **Masterclass Tab & Audio:** [Practice While My Guitar Gently Weeps](https://musicscene.com.au/lessons/songs/song-analysis/while-my-guitar-gently-weeps)

### 2. Don't Look Back in Anger — Oasis
* **Key & Tempo:** C Major • 84 BPM
* **The Secret:** Replacing Em with a dominant E7 (V of vi) injects a sharp G# leading tone that charges the progression into Am. Noel follows this in the pre-chorus with a borrowed minor iv chord (Fm) that gives the stadium ballad its tear-jerking soul.
* **Masterclass Tab & Audio:** [Practice Don't Look Back in Anger](https://musicscene.com.au/lessons/songs/song-analysis/dont-look-back-in-anger)

### 3. What's My Scene — Hoodoo Gurus
* **Key & Tempo:** E Major • 124 BPM
* **The Secret:** Iconic high-register double stops over a ringing open E pedal point, followed by an ascending scalar chord climb (F#m → G#m → A → B) right before the solo kicks in.
* **Masterclass Tab & Audio:** [Practice What's My Scene](https://musicscene.com.au/lessons/songs/song-analysis/whats-my-scene)

### 4. The Wind Cries Mary — Jimi Hendrix
* **Key & Tempo:** F Major • 80 BPM
* **The Secret:** Parallel chromatic sliding triads (Eb → E → F) combined with thumb-over chord grips that free up your fingers for sweet Curtis Mayfield-inspired hammer-ons.
* **Masterclass Tab & Audio:** [Practice The Wind Cries Mary](https://musicscene.com.au/lessons/songs/song-analysis/the-wind-cries-mary)

### 5. Plug In Baby — Muse
* **Key & Tempo:** B Minor • 136 BPM
* **The Secret:** A neoclassical arpeggio tour-de-force that opens on the dominant F# chord, highlighting the exotic A# leading tone from B Harmonic Minor before crushing into fuzz-drenched power chords.
* **Masterclass Tab & Audio:** [Practice Plug In Baby](https://musicscene.com.au/lessons/songs/song-analysis/plug-in-baby)

## 💡 Theory Secret of the Week: Borrowed Minor iv (Fm in C Major)
Why does shifting from F to Fm in a C major progression tug on your heartstrings every single time?
* F Major is built from **F - A - C**.
* F Minor lowers the third to **F - Ab - C**.
That **Ab** note is borrowed from the parallel natural minor (C Aeolian). When an upbeat, sun-drenched major progression suddenly introduces a dark minor 6th degree, your ear experiences an immediate shock of nostalgia, vulnerability, and beauty before resolving back home to C.

## ⚡ Tone Stack of the Week: British Edge-of-Breakup Bloom
Dial in your tube amplifier or modeling preset for singing sustain without fizzy distortion:
* **Gain:** `6.5`
* **Treble:** `6.5`
* **Middle:** `7.0`
* **Bass:** `5.0`
* **Presence:** `6.0`

> **Pro Tip:** Keep your amp gain locked at 6.5. When playing verses, roll your guitar's volume knob down to **7.0** for crystal-clear articulate chime. When you step up for the solo, roll it to **10** for instant saturation and singing feedback!

## 🎯 Weekly 5-Minute Practice Challenge: The Line Cliché
1. Anchor your 1st and 2nd fingers on fret 1 (B string) and fret 2 (D string) for a standard Am triad.
2. Step down the bass notes on beats 1 and 3: **Open A → 3rd fret low E (G) → 2nd fret low E (F#) → 1st fret low E (F)**.
3. Keep the high open E string ringing clean on every single pass.
4. Run for 5 minutes uninterrupted at **50 BPM**, then bump to **66 BPM**.
